Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

What we collect

When you request a quote, we collect the details you type into the form: your name, business name (if any), email, phone number, city, and any optional details about your space, schedule and how you heard about us.

To block spam, we also record a scrambled (hashed) version of your internet address, and our spam-check service checks your browser. We also note which page you first landed on and the site that sent you, so we know which advertising works.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your request: to contact you, schedule a walkthrough and prepare your quote. We do not sell your information or share it for anyone else's marketing.

Who helps us run the site

  • Cloudflare hosts this website, stores quote requests, runs the spam check and provides basic visit statistics without cookies.
  • Resend delivers the email that notifies our team about your request.

These companies handle your information on our behalf and only to provide these services.

How long we keep it

If you don't become a customer, we delete your request after 24 months without activity. If you become a customer, we keep your contact details for as long as we work together.

Cookies

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The spam check may use technical data that's needed for it to work.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time. Email safeguardcleaningco@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.
