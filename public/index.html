Questions, answered

How much does cleaning cost?+

It depends on your building: square footage, restrooms, layout and what you need done. That's why we don't post a price list. After your walkthrough, you get a quote built for your space.

Is the walkthrough really free?+

Yes. The walkthrough and the quote are both free.

What areas do you serve?+

The entire Kansas City metro, including Overland Park, Kansas. We're based in Blue Springs, Missouri.

Do you clean homes?+

Yes. Businesses are our focus, but we clean homes on request. Just choose "My home" on the quote form.
